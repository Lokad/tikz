Braced size lists above enumerate individual filenames. Resources are embedded
in the assembly and distributed as JSON under font-data/ in the NuGet package.
The provenance and profile are distributed there too. Independent metric,
atlas and radical fixtures under tests/Reference/MathFonts/ are further
font-derived development data with the same license; they are not components
of the shipped font-data work.

Changes from Latin Modern: fixed font/size subset, JSON metrics and math font
parameters, flattened pair rules, preservation of larger-glyph links and
extensible recipes, and baseline-relative SVG outline extraction rounded to
six decimal places. Missing TFM characters stay missing. The 12pt symbols use
the 10pt design scaled to 12pt; the extension face stays fixed at 10pt.
Font faces and resource filenames use the distinct LokadTikzMath name.
No installed font is replaced; no original author's support is implied.

The complete original work, including Type 1 sources and metrics, is available:
  https://ctan.org/tex-archive/fonts/lm
