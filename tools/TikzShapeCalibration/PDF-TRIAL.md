# Prospective ordinary-PDF policy trial

```text
node tools/TikzVisual/shape-pdf-policy-self-test.mjs
node tools/TikzVisual/trial-shape-pdf.mjs
```

The first command runs 38 pure comparator/policy controls without TeX, a
browser or the converter. The second includes those controls, runs a fresh
[independent PDF calibration](PDF-CALIBRATION.md), fixes two separate
prospective policies, replays the independently measured images and only then
invokes the existing public primary/numeric/frame trial.

The original driver and independently reconstructed native semantic-root SVG
have separate limits against the same ordinary PDF page. Each limit belongs
to one representation, region, scale, natural/expanded frame and white
background. No other admitted fixture supplies a tolerance, and the plus/minus
scaled-point sensitivity variants authorize no margin. Foreground means are
rounded upward to 1e-9 channel units; other maxima are exact observations.

The metrics include raw and premultiplied changed-pixel counts, maximum channel
errors, mean error over every visible foreground sample, component-count
difference, symmetric boundary displacement and foreground-pixel count
difference. A maximum channel error of 255 is retained when independently
observed; it cannot by itself establish a useful match. The other whole/local
gates remain required. Missing edge sets have explicit states and null
distances. They fail whenever the independent region had measurable edges.
Faint below-threshold content is still covered by channel/foreground metrics.

Both positive PDF gates require natural and expanded framing at 1x/4x. The
native page/camera, 99 required regions, integer crops and white background
are fixed before comparisons. Reference foreground/edge/component counts
cannot change. Stable separately decoded PDF/SVG repeats and zero differences
outside the whole-picture crop are also required.

Combined trials require nonempty numeric, ink/root, original-SVG primary,
original-SVG/PDF and candidate-SVG/PDF evidence. Missing views, regions,
metrics, changed crops, false summaries and empty prerequisites cannot pass.
A passing trial still has overall verdict pending and approvedImagePolicy
false. These are prospective fixture-only limits awaiting review and immutable
admission.

## Sensitivity and inspectable evidence

Ten mutations operate only on exported SVG artifacts: missing period, missing
changed-font glyph, missing/doubled border, shifted glyph, half-opacity glyph,
quarter-pixel whole-picture shift, height crop, added ink and added ink outside
the whole-picture camera. Numeric gates are bypassed for these controls. Each
must be rejected at both natural white scales. The pure controls independently
prove that whole-picture limits can accept missing faint punctuation/strokes
while fixed local crops reject them. Synthetic controls are comparator tests
and make no ordinary-PDF or TikZ compatibility claim.

Fresh GUID directories under artifacts/node-shape-pdf-trial/ retain complete
and incomplete experiments. latest.json points to a report/hash rather than
silently replacing historical results. The bundle owns exact tool sources,
command output, prospective policies, oracle/frame/page evidence, public scene
and source spans, the complete fresh primary bundle, original/mutated SVGs,
whole/page PNGs, paired crops, overlays and raw/amplified differences. All owned
artifacts are hash-checked. Representation controls explicitly compare public
and independent native page PNG bytes; neither representation is fitted to the
other image.

The report's agent entry point links the source, viewer, numeric/frame reports,
policy, focused rerun and unexpected cases. Crop attribution follows the
unmutated public scene; overlapping regions do not alone prove which operation
caused a mismatch.

Exit zero means all expected trial outcomes; one retains a candidate/control
failure; two denotes infrastructure/provenance/rendering failure. Failure
evidence stays intact. Unexpected outcomes require investigation; they do not
authorize widening a limit from candidate errors. Reviewed immutable admission,
focused and interaction corpora, and hosted execution remain outstanding.
The production library and existing admitted references/policies are unchanged.

## Executed result

The local run passes all 13 expected cases: original-driver replay, native
replay, public candidate, and ten defects. It retains 32 PDF views and 3,264
region/page/repeat gates. The combined public route passes 4,461 native numeric
checks, 406 ink/root checks, 1,188 primary whole/local gates and 408 gates on
each PDF side. All four candidate page PNGs are byte-identical to the
independently reconstructed native page PNGs. The original-driver/PDF raw
differences remain present; these results do not claim pixel-identical PDF
rendering.

The added outside-camera ink at 4x passes the whole/local image limits and is
rejected only by the required full-page check. At 1x the expanded natural root
also changes clipping observations inside the whole region. Both outcomes are
retained rather than normalizing the mutated image to its content. Paired
missing-period and root-crop 4x images have been inspected.

The completed bundle owns 1,647 artifacts, including 1,570 PNGs and the copied
fresh primary evidence. The pure policy controls and all 212 ordinary tests
pass. Visual CI includes the pure controls; the full research trial remains
an explicit local command. Hosted execution and reviewed admission are pending.
An independent final audit rederives both policies, recomputes all 3,264
evaluations, checks every owned artifact hash and verifies 20 tool hashes
against the current files and exact staged Git bytes.
